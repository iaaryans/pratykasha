How It Works

From setup to seamless attendance — everything flows intelligently.

1

Server Setup & Location

Admin registers the institute and pins the exact geolocation to enable smart attendance fencing.

2

Add Employees

Admins add employees by name, date of birth and facial data into the system for identity verification.

3

Employee Login & Face Auth

Employees log in using their unique ID and undergo quick AI-based facial recognition for secure entry.

4

Attendance Confirmed

If geolocation and face match criteria are met, attendance is automatically recorded and updated live.
